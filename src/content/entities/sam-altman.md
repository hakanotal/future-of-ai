CEO of [OpenAI](/e/openai), which he co-founded in 2015 with [Elon Musk](/e/elon-musk), [Greg Brockman](/e/greg-brockman), [Ilya Sutskever](/e/ilya-sutskever) and others to ensure artificial general intelligence "benefits all of humanity".

He credits the rationalists with the idea. In February 2023 he wrote that [Eliezer Yudkowsky](/e/eliezer-yudkowsky) had "done more to accelerate AGI than anyone else", had "got many of us interested in AGI" and was "critical in the decision to start openai", adding that Yudkowsky might one day deserve the Nobel Peace Prize for it. In November 2023 the nonprofit board removed him for not being "consistently candid", and within five days employees and Microsoft had brought him back ([board crisis](/e/openai-board-crisis)). In 2025 he led OpenAI's conversion into a public benefit corporation ([recapitalization](/e/openai-recapitalization)). Gebru and Torres name him among the figures linked to more than one TESCREAL ideology. He is the clearest case of a single person holding the question of [who decides](/e/ai-governance) in practice.
