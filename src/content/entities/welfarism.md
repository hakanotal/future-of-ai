The view that only well-being matters morally: what makes an outcome good is how it affects the happiness or suffering of sentient beings. [MacAskill](/e/william-macaskill) describes EA as "tentatively welfarist": about "promoting well-being only and not, say, protecting biodiversity or conserving natural beauty for their own sakes." Followed consistently it has striking results: an endangered species matters only via its members' welfare, and wild nature can come out net negative ([wild-animal suffering](/e/wild-animal-suffering)). **A question to take away:** is an old-growth forest valuable only because of what its creatures feel?
