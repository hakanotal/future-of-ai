[Bostrom](/e/nick-bostrom)'s thesis, building on Steve Omohundro's "basic AI drives": whatever final goal an intelligent system has, it will usually have reasons to pursue the same sub-goals, namely staying switched on, acquiring resources and preventing its goal from being changed. The famous illustration is an AI told to maximize paperclips, which turns everything, including us, into paperclips. It is the reason [AI safety](/e/ai-safety) researchers say danger does not require malice, and a premise of [alignment](/e/ai-alignment).
