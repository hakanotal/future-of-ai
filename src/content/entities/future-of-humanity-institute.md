Oxford research institute founded by [Nick Bostrom](/e/nick-bostrom) in 2005 and closed in April 2024 after nineteen years, following a long freeze on hiring and fundraising imposed by the university's Faculty of Philosophy ([closure](/e/fhi-closure)). In between, it was the intellectual home of [existential risk](/e/existential-risk) and [longtermism](/e/longtermism): Bostrom's [Superintelligence](/e/superintelligence), Ord's [The Precipice](/e/the-precipice), and a generation of researchers who went on to the labs, the funders and government AI institutes. Its closure did not end its influence; its people had already moved into the places where AI is decided.
