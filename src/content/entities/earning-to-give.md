[MacAskill's](/e/william-macaskill) pitch to quantitatively gifted students: your career is your biggest moral decision, and you may do more good by *not* working at a charity, go to Wall Street, earn enormously, give most of it away. [Bankman-Fried](/e/sam-bankman-fried) heard it at MIT and redirected from animal welfare to [Jane Street](/e/jane-street); [80,000 Hours](/e/eighty-thousand-hours) marketed him as the exemplar.

Downstream the logic stretches: [morally controversial careers](/e/moral-offsetting) justified by donations, and the [replaceability](/e/replaceability) argument that choosing the indigent-defending job over the corporate one effectively kills the people your salary could have saved. The recurring structure: individually plausible steps to a conclusion ordinary intuition rejects, with the rejection read as irrationality.
