California's Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act, signed by Governor Newsom on 29 September 2025: the first US law aimed specifically at frontier-AI safety. Large developers must publish their safety frameworks, report critical safety incidents, and protect whistleblowers. It followed the [veto of SB 1047](/e/sb-1047-veto), a tougher liability bill, and took a lighter touch. Within months, a [federal executive order](/e/state-preemption-order) set out to challenge state AI laws in the name of national competitiveness. Who governs frontier AI in the US, the states or Washington, is now itself contested.
