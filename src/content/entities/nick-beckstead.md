Philosopher whose 2013 Rutgers [dissertation](/e/beckstead-dissertation-2013) was an early systematic case for [longtermism](/e/longtermism). He was a research fellow at the [Future of Humanity Institute](/e/future-of-humanity-institute), a program officer at [Open Philanthropy](/e/open-philanthropy), and CEO of the [FTX Future Fund](/e/ftx-future-fund) until its team resigned on 10 November 2022. TIME reported that he was among the EA leaders warned about Bankman-Fried in 2018 ([Alameda split](/e/alameda-2018-split)). His dissertation's hedged claim that saving a rich-country life may matter more for the far future, "other things being equal", is the most quoted, and most often misquoted, sentence in the debate.
