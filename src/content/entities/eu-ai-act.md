The European Union's AI Act, the world's first comprehensive AI law. It sorts AI uses by risk, bans a few outright (such as social scoring), sets duties for high-risk systems in areas like hiring, education and border control, and since 2 August 2025 has placed obligations on providers of general-purpose models. Its spirit is closer to [AI ethics](/e/ai-ethics) (rights, transparency, bias) than to existential risk. In 2026 the EU's "digital omnibus" delayed parts of it amid pressure over competitiveness. It is the strongest example so far of [who decides](/e/ai-governance) meaning elected lawmakers, and of how hard that is to sustain during a [race](/e/ai-race).
