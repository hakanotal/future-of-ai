Former director of IARPA, the US intelligence community's research agency, who became the founding director of Georgetown's [CSET](/e/cset) in 2019 (launched with $55 million from Open Philanthropy), later served in the Biden White House, and in 2022 became CEO of RAND. He was a founding trustee of Anthropic's [Long-Term Benefit Trust](/e/long-term-benefit-trust) until December 2023. His career traces the route by which EA-funded concern about AI risk entered the US national-security establishment ([AI security](/e/ai-security)).
