Charity evaluator founded in 2007 by [Karnofsky](/e/holden-karnofsky) and [Hassenfeld](/e/elie-hassenfeld) to judge interventions by outcomes, not overhead ratios. Its top-charity list, bed nets (Against Malaria Foundation, Malaria Consortium), vitamin A (Helen Keller International), vaccine incentives (New Incentives), channeled hundreds of millions; its rough price is $3,500–$5,500 per averted death, presented as approximation, not price tag.

[Kalulu's](/e/anthony-kalulu) critique cuts here: not one top charity is African though much work happens in Africa, and ~1% of anti-poverty funding reaches Southern grassroots directly. GiveWell's reply: top charities work through giants like the Global Fund because that remains the most measurable way to save lives, which restates the disagreement rather than rebutting it.
