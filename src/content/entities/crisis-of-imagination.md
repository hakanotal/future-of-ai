The activist [Rob Hopkins](/e/rob-hopkins)'s diagnosis: "Given everything that humanity has accomplished, all of it driven by leaps of imagination, why is envisioning a safer, saner, more peaceful path forward so consistently beyond our reach?" His sharper worry is that imagination has been captured, "increasingly harnessed to create demand for things nobody really needs… almost as if imagination has been co-opted in the service of our own extinction." It pairs with Benjamin's [ustopia](/e/ustopia): the future is not only something to predict, but something to picture, and to decide.
