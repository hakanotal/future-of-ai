Inventor and futurist (born 1948) who made the technological singularity a mainstream idea. [The Singularity Is Near](/e/singularity-is-near) (2005) predicted human-level AI by 2029 and a merger of humans and machines around 2045; he co-founded [Singularity University](/e/singularity-university) in 2008, co-ran the [Singularity Summit](/e/singularity-summit), and joined Google in 2012.

He moved in the early [extropian](/e/extropianism) milieu and remains [singularitarianism](/e/singularitarianism)'s best-known advocate, though he did not coin the word. The scholar [Robert Geraci](/e/robert-geraci) reads his work as [apocalyptic theology](/e/apocalyptic-ai) in secular dress. Kurzweil's own motives are unusually candid: he has spoken for decades about hoping to overcome death, including, one day, recreating his late father from data. That is Fedorov's [cosmist](/e/cosmism) dream in the language of Silicon Valley.
