The February 2026 summit in New Delhi, the first in the international series hosted by a Global South country. Its declaration, endorsed by 92 countries and international organizations, centered on "welfare for all", access and development, and India announced a major expansion of public "sovereign" computing capacity. Its framing asked a question the earlier summits had rarely foregrounded: [who decides](/e/ai-governance), and for which majority of the world, what AI is for?
