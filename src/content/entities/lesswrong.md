Community blog and forum founded by [Eliezer Yudkowsky](/e/eliezer-yudkowsky) in 2009, growing out of the group blog [Overcoming Bias](/e/overcoming-bias). It is the home of [rationalism](/e/rationalism): the [Sequences](/e/sequences), debates on decision theory, and years of early writing on [AI alignment](/e/ai-alignment) before the labs existed. It is now run by [Oliver Habryka](/e/oliver-habryka)'s Lightcone. Many ideas that later shaped the AI industry were first argued here, in public, by pseudonymous posters. That made it one of the more open places where AI's future was debated, and also one of the least representative.
