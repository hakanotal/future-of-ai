Chess prodigy, game designer and neuroscientist who co-founded [DeepMind](/e/deepmind) in 2010 with [Shane Legg](/e/shane-legg) and [Mustafa Suleyman](/e/mustafa-suleyman), with the mission to "solve intelligence". After speaking at the 2010 [Singularity Summit](/e/singularity-summit), he won over [Peter Thiel](/e/peter-thiel) by opening with chess rather than a pitch. Google bought DeepMind in 2014; Hassabis shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for AlphaFold. He has been reported comparing the AGI effort to the Manhattan Project (no verbatim source found; see the verification log), and he is one of a handful of people whose choices shape what AI becomes.
