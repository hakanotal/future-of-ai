The observation, made by [Émile P. Torres](/e/emile-torres) and [Ruha Benjamin](/e/ruha-benjamin), that talk about AI keeps swinging between two stories: machines will bring paradise, or they will bring extinction. In both, technology acts on us and the humans making choices disappear. And both crowd out the present: whether the future is heaven or hell, the question of who is being harmed right now, and by whom, never gets asked. Benjamin's alternative is [ustopia](/e/ustopia).
