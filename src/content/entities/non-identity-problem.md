[Derek Parfit](/e/derek-parfit)'s puzzle: our choices about energy, war or even when to have children determine *which* people will exist in the future. So how can a policy harm future people, if without it those particular people would never have been born? Parfit concluded that we can still act wrongly toward the future even when no particular person is made worse off. That conclusion is a [premise](/e/longtermism) of longtermism: the future's claim on us doesn't depend on knowing who will be there.
