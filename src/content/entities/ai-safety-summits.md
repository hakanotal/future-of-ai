The intergovernmental summit series that began at Bletchley Park in November 2023, where 28 countries and the EU signed a declaration on frontier-AI risks and the UK and US announced safety institutes. At Seoul in May 2024, frontier labs signed voluntary safety commitments. At the [Paris AI Action Summit](/e/paris-ai-action-summit) in February 2025, the US and UK refused to sign the final declaration, and the US vice president warned against over-regulation. At the [India AI Impact Summit](/e/india-ai-impact-summit) in February 2026, the first in the Global South, 92 countries and organizations endorsed a declaration framed around "welfare for all". Read the names in order (safety, action, impact) and you can watch the question of [who decides](/e/ai-governance), and about what, shift.
