The [welfarist](/e/welfarism) worry that most wild animals, most of which die young from starvation, predation or disease, live lives with more suffering than joy. Some EA writers, including MacAskill in [What We Owe the Future](/e/what-we-owe-the-future), take seriously the possibility that wild nature is net negative in welfare terms, which would make the loss of wild habitat less bad, or even good. Critics treat this as a reductio: a framework that can conclude that destroying ecosystems is an improvement has lost track of something. Defenders reply that it is honest to follow the argument where it goes, and that nobody is proposing to act on it.
