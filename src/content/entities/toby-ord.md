Australian-born Oxford philosopher (born 1979) who, as a young academic, calculated that giving away much of a lecturer's salary over a lifetime could save thousands of lives, and then did it. In 2009 he co-founded [Giving What We Can](/e/give-what-we-can) with [William MacAskill](/e/william-macaskill) and [Bernadette Young](/e/bernadette-young); its members pledge at least 10% of their income.

Ord asked the question Singer had not: not *whether* to give but *where*, since charities differ in effectiveness by orders of magnitude. Around 2017 he and MacAskill coined [longtermism](/e/longtermism). His [The Precipice](/e/the-precipice) (2020), written at the [Future of Humanity Institute](/e/future-of-humanity-institute), estimated a 1-in-6 chance of existential catastrophe this century and proposed [existential security](/e/existential-security) and a [Long Reflection](/e/long-reflection). He is a useful reminder that the movement's founders began with bed nets, and that the pivot to the far future was an argument, not a betrayal.
