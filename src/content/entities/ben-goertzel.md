AI researcher (born 1966) who helped popularize the term "artificial general intelligence" and later founded SingularityNET. His 2010 [Cosmist Manifesto](/e/cosmist-manifesto) revived the word "cosmism" for a modern, technological version of [Nikolai Fedorov](/e/nikolai-fedorov)'s dream: "space-time engineering and future magic will permit achieving by scientific means most of the promises of religions", including resurrecting the dead "by copying them to the future". It is the bundle's most openly religious text, and the plainest example of [techno-millenarianism](/e/techno-millenarianism).
