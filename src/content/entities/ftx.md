Crypto exchange founded in 2019 by [Sam Bankman-Fried](/e/sam-bankman-fried), based first in Hong Kong and then the Bahamas: low fees, Super Bowl ads, Tom Brady, the Miami Heat's arena. At its peak it was valued at $32 billion. It filed for bankruptcy on 11 November 2022 with about $8 billion of customer money missing, much of it routed to its sister firm [Alameda Research](/e/alameda-research) ([collapse](/e/ftx-collapse)).

Its philanthropy was EA's great hope: the [FTX Future Fund](/e/ftx-future-fund) and a $500 million investment in [Anthropic](/e/anthropic). Its bankruptcy CEO, [John J. Ray III](/e/john-j-ray-iii), said he had never seen "such a complete failure of corporate controls". In a final irony, the estate [sold the Anthropic stake](/e/ftx-anthropic-stake-sale) for about $1.3 billion in 2024, and the rise of an AI company helped repay the people FTX had defrauded.
