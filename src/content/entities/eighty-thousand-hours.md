Career-advice organization founded in 2011 by [William MacAskill](/e/william-macaskill) and [Benjamin Todd](/e/benjamin-todd), named for the roughly 80,000 hours in a working life. It popularized [earning to give](/e/earning-to-give) and the [ITN framework](/e/itn-framework) for choosing causes. It has ranked risks from AI as the world's most pressing problem since 2016; in April 2025 it refocused on helping people navigate the transition to AGI, and in 2025 it placed climate change among "other pressing problems" rather than at the top. Its rankings quietly shape where thousands of talented people spend their careers, which makes them a form of governance too.
