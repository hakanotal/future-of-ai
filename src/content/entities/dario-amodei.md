Physicist and AI researcher who spent nearly five years at [OpenAI](/e/openai), rising to VP of Research, before leaving in 2020–21 with his sister [Daniela Amodei](/e/daniela-amodei) and colleagues to found [Anthropic](/e/anthropic), where he is CEO.

The split was over speed and safety: with Microsoft's billions arriving, the founders believed OpenAI was tilting toward commercialization. Their answer was not to stop building but to build differently, as a public benefit corporation with a [Long-Term Benefit Trust](/e/long-term-benefit-trust) and a [Responsible Scaling Policy](/e/responsible-scaling-policy), on the argument that a safety-focused lab has to be at the frontier to influence it. Critics call that the [race](/e/ai-race) in a more thoughtful costume. In 2026 his company's refusal to let the Pentagon use its models for autonomous weapons and mass surveillance became the [dispute](/e/anthropic-pentagon-dispute) that tested whether a lab's safety lines can hold against a state.
