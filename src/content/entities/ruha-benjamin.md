Princeton sociologist of race, justice and technology, author of *Race After Technology* (2019) and [Imagination: A Manifesto](/e/imagination-a-manifesto) (2024). She argues that a small sliver of humanity is imposing its visions of the future on everyone else: space travel, superintelligence, bunkers. Their imaginations "grow limp", she says, when it comes to transforming social reality. Against the [utopia/apocalypse binary](/e/utopia-apocalypse-binary) she proposes [ustopia](/e/ustopia), borrowed from Margaret Atwood: what we create together "when we're wide awake". Her work is this site's clearest answer to [who should decide](/e/ai-governance).
