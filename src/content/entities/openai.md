AI lab founded in December 2015 as a nonprofit by [Sam Altman](/e/sam-altman), [Elon Musk](/e/elon-musk), [Greg Brockman](/e/greg-brockman), [Ilya Sutskever](/e/ilya-sutskever) and others, with the mission to ensure that artificial general intelligence "benefits all of humanity". Its founders' fears came from [Bostrom](/e/nick-bostrom) and the [rationalists](/e/rationalism): Altman credits [Yudkowsky](/e/eliezer-yudkowsky) as "critical in the decision to start openai". [Open Philanthropy](/e/open-philanthropy) gave it $30 million in 2017 and a board seat.

The mission ran into money. A capped-profit arm (2019) took about $13 billion from [Microsoft](/e/microsoft); key safety researchers left to found [Anthropic](/e/anthropic) (2021) and, later, after the Superalignment team's collapse in 2024 ([Jan Leike](/e/jan-leike): safety had "taken a backseat to shiny products"). The nonprofit board fired Altman in 2023 and lost ([board crisis](/e/openai-board-crisis)). In 2025 it became a public benefit corporation controlled by the OpenAI Foundation ([recapitalization](/e/openai-recapitalization)). Its path is the clearest record we have of what happens to a lab's safety governance under the pressure of the [race](/e/ai-race). Critics add the labor underneath, from Kenyan data labelers at [Sama](/e/sama) to the story told in [Empire of AI](/e/empire-of-ai).
