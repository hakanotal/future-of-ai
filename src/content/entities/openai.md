AI lab founded 2015, originally nonprofit, on existential-risk concerns from this community's orbit, [Yudkowsky](/e/eliezer-yudkowsky) got many of them into AGI, [Bostrom](/e/nick-bostrom) gave the concerns academic cover, [Musk](/e/elon-musk) and [Altman](/e/sam-altman) supplied the founding energy. Microsoft's ~$13B partnership then tilted it from safety toward commercialization, the grievance behind the [Anthropic](/e/anthropic) walkout.

The [November 2023 board crisis](/e/openai-board-crisis) exposed the movement's boardroom weakness: the EA-sympathetic directors ([Toner](/e/helen-toner), [McCauley](/e/tasha-mccauley), [D'Angelo](/e/adam-dangelo), [Sutskever](/e/ilya-sutskever)) fired [Altman](/e/sam-altman), 745 of 770 employees revolted, and five days later the safety camp was effectively removed.
