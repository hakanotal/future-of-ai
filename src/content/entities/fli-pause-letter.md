"Pause Giant AI Experiments", the [Future of Life Institute](/e/future-of-life-institute)'s open letter of 22 March 2023, calling on all labs to pause training systems more powerful than GPT-4 for at least six months. [Elon Musk](/e/elon-musk) and thousands of researchers signed. No lab paused. A week later [Yudkowsky](/e/yudkowsky-time-oped-2023) replied in TIME that a pause "isn't enough". The letter is a measure of how little the AI-safety world could make happen by persuasion alone: it could fill a news cycle but not stop the [race](/e/ai-race).
