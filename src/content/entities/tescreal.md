TESCREAL names a bundle of seven ideologies that travel together through Silicon Valley institutions: [Transhumanism](/e/transhumanism), [Extropianism](/e/extropianism), [Singularitarianism](/e/singularitarianism), [Cosmism](/e/cosmism), [Rationalism](/e/rationalism), [Effective Altruism](/e/effective-altruism) and [Longtermism](/e/longtermism).

The term was coined by [Timnit Gebru](/e/timnit-gebru) and [Émile P. Torres](/e/emile-torres) in 2023 and developed in [The TESCREAL Bundle](/e/tescreal-bundle-paper) (2024). Their claim is genealogical rather than doctrinal: these schools share roots, personnel and funding, even where their members disagree with each other, [Yudkowsky's doomerism](/e/eliezer-yudkowsky) and [Andreessen's accelerationism](/e/marc-andreessen) are in open conflict, yet both agree building as fast as possible is the correct response.

Read the bundle as a map of intellectual genealogy and funding networks, not as an accusation that any individual believes all seven things. Its sharpest use, per Gebru: the language of [existential threat](/e/existential-risk) legitimizes building dangerous, unscoped systems, while today's concrete harms, the domain of [AI ethics](/e/ai-ethics), stay invisible in the shadow of a speculative future.
