AI company founded January 2021 by seven ex-OpenAI researchers led by [Dario](/e/dario-amodei) and [Daniela Amodei](/e/daniela-amodei), with [Kaplan](/e/jared-kaplan), [Clark](/e/jack-clark), [Olah](/e/chris-olah), [Mann](/e/ben-mann), [McCandlish](/e/sam-mccandlish) and [Brown](/e/tom-brown). A public benefit corporation with an unusual guardrail: the [Long-Term Benefit Trust](/e/long-term-benefit-trust), designed to elect the board and hold the mission.

Before the collapse, [FTX](/e/ftx) put $500M in, likely customer money. The estate's $884M sale in March 2024 partly repaid creditors. Since then Amazon (~$10.75B) and Google (~$3.3B) dominate the cap table, which is the live question about the Trust's independence. [Karnofsky](/e/holden-karnofsky) joined in January 2025.
