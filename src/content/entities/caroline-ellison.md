Stanford-trained mathematician, former [Jane Street](/e/jane-street) trader and CEO of [Alameda Research](/e/alameda-research), and for a period Bankman-Fried's romantic partner. At [his trial](/e/us-v-bankman-fried) in October 2023 she was the star cooperating witness, testifying that he directed the use of FTX customer funds to cover Alameda's losses and investments. She was sentenced to two years in 2024. Her testimony turned the story from "a crypto firm failed" into "a crime was committed".
