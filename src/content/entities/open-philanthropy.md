The [Karnofsky](/e/holden-karnofsky)–[Moskovitz](/e/dustin-moskovitz)–[Tuna](/e/cari-tuna) grantmaker founded in 2014 on [Good Ventures](/e/good-ventures) money, renamed Coefficient Giving in 2025, with over $4 billion deployed across global health and catastrophic risk.

The research question that matters most about it is the split: year by year, how much went to longtermist x-risk versus global health and development? That division is the empirical test of the claim that money moved from the present to the future, and the dataset to answer it is largely downloadable.
