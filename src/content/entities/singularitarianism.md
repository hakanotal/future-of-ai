The belief, popularized by [Ray Kurzweil](/e/ray-kurzweil), in a coming technological singularity, the point where progress becomes unstoppable and incomprehensible to unaided minds.

[Torres](/e/emile-torres) separates two readings: rate (a century of change compressed into seconds) and the [intelligence explosion](/e/intelligence-explosion), recursive self-improvement where AGI designs AGI+, reaching superintelligence in days. The viral [AI 2027](/e/ai-2027) scenario is this theory in contemporary dress: hundreds of thousands of superhuman researcher copies within years.
