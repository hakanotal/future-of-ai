AI lab founded 2010 with [Thiel's](/e/peter-thiel) $2.25M seed from the [Singularity Summit](/e/singularity-summit) network; [Hassabis](/e/demis-hassabis) described the ten-year plan as a Manhattan Project for AI. Its safety teams are full of researchers shaped by this community's concerns.

DeepMind matters here as the prototype: the path from extropian living rooms to Thiel checks to a frontier lab, years before OpenAI or Anthropic existed. The same genealogy, the same money, earlier.
