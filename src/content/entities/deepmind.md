AI lab founded in London in 2010 by [Demis Hassabis](/e/demis-hassabis), [Shane Legg](/e/shane-legg) and [Mustafa Suleyman](/e/mustafa-suleyman), with the mission to "solve intelligence, and then use that to solve everything else". It was born out of the [Singularity Summit](/e/singularity-summit) network: after the 2010 summit, Hassabis won over [Peter Thiel](/e/peter-thiel), its first major investor; [Jaan Tallinn](/e/jaan-tallinn) also invested early; and Sam Altman credits [Yudkowsky](/e/eliezer-yudkowsky) with helping "deepmind get funded". Google bought it in 2014. Its AlphaGo (2016) and AlphaFold (Nobel Prize, 2024) are among AI's great achievements; its existence is also the first move in the [race](/e/ai-race) that the rest of this story follows.
