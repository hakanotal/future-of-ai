The idea that an AI able to improve AI could trigger runaway recursive self-improvement: AGI designs AGI+, which designs AGI++, until within days or hours the result is far beyond human intelligence. It was first sketched by the statistician I. J. Good in 1965 and is central to [singularitarianism](/e/singularitarianism) and to [doomerism](/e/doomerism), since a takeoff that fast leaves no second chance. [AI 2027](/e/ai-2027) turned it into a dated scenario. **A question to take away:** if you believed the takeoff could happen in a weekend, who would you want holding the off switch, and would you trust that there was one?
