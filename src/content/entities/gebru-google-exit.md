In December 2020 Google forced out [Timnit Gebru](/e/timnit-gebru), co-lead of its Ethical AI team, after a dispute over the paper that became [On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots](/e/stochastic-parrots). Google said she resigned; she said she was fired. Thousands of Google employees and researchers signed letters in her support. Her co-lead [Margaret Mitchell](/e/margaret-mitchell) was fired in February 2021. The episode showed that a company's in-house ethics research could be overruled by the company. A year later Gebru founded [DAIR](/e/dair) outside it. It is an early lesson in [who decides](/e/ai-governance).
