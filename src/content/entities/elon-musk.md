Entrepreneur whose projects touch more TESCREAL letters than almost anyone's: [SpaceX](/e/spacex) and the Mars plan ([cosmism](/e/cosmism)), [Neuralink](/e/neuralink) ([transhumanism](/e/transhumanism)), and a long public engagement with AI risk.

After reading Bostrom's [Superintelligence](/e/superintelligence) he began calling AI humanity's biggest existential threat. In 2015 he gave the [Future of Life Institute](/e/future-of-life-institute) $10 million for AI-safety research and co-founded [OpenAI](/e/openai), leaving in 2018. He called MacAskill's [What We Owe the Future](/e/what-we-owe-the-future) "a close match for my philosophy", signed the 2023 [pause letter](/e/fli-pause-letter), and has described humans as a "biological bootloader" for digital superintelligence ([AI succession](/e/ai-succession)). He also founded a rival AI company and sued to stop OpenAI's [recapitalization](/e/openai-recapitalization). Warning about AI, funding its safety, building it, and suing competitors: his career is the bundle's contradictions in one person.
