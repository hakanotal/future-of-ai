Scottish philosopher, co-founder of [Giving What We Can](/e/give-what-we-can), [80,000 Hours](/e/eighty-thousand-hours) and the [Centre for Effective Altruism](/e/centre-for-effective-altruism), who widened the frame from *how much to give* to *how to live to do the most good*, career, time, diet, everything in the calculation.

His 2022 [What We Owe the Future](/e/what-we-owe-the-future) moved the movement's center of gravity from today's poor to tomorrow's trillions. He sold [Bankman-Fried](/e/sam-bankman-fried) on [earning to give](/e/earning-to-give), sat on the [Future Fund](/e/ftx-future-fund)'s advisory team, and after the collapse published a genuine retraction: sincere-seeming moral signals do not imply moral integrity. Among longtermists he counts as a moderate, which is exactly what alarms the critics.
