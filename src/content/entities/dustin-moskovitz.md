Facebook co-founder whose [Good Ventures](/e/good-ventures) foundation with [Cari Tuna](/e/cari-tuna) funds [Open Philanthropy](/e/open-philanthropy), EA's largest living donors. After FTX he went quiet for a period, deactivating social accounts.

His money kept flowing while the movement's momentum stopped: Open Philanthropy's spend rose through 2024–25 even as the [Future Fund](/e/ftx-future-fund)'s ~$160M in commitments evaporated. Reconciling those two facts, what was lost versus what continued, is the post-FTX financial reality check the research backlog demands.
