Facebook co-founder (born 1984) who, with his wife [Cari Tuna](/e/cari-tuna), became effective altruism's largest living donor through [Good Ventures](/e/good-ventures) and [Open Philanthropy](/e/open-philanthropy). By 2025 their giving had directed more than $4 billion, across global health, animal welfare and catastrophic risks, including a large share of all philanthropic money for AI safety.

When FTX's money vanished in 2022, "EA lost its money" became a common headline. That was misleading: the Moskovitz–Tuna funding continued, which left the movement *more* dependent on a single couple's priorities. In November 2025 Open Philanthropy renamed itself Coefficient Giving and opened funds to other donors. Gebru and Torres name Moskovitz among the figures linked to more than one TESCREAL ideology. For the question of [who decides](/e/ai-governance), he is a clear case: in philanthropy, what counts as a problem is often decided by whoever pays for the research.
