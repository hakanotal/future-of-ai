AI alignment researcher who led language-model alignment at [OpenAI](/e/openai) and helped develop reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), the technique that makes chatbots follow instructions. He founded the [Alignment Research Center](/e/alignment-research-center) in 2021, sat on Anthropic's [Long-Term Benefit Trust](/e/long-term-benefit-trust) from 2023, and in April 2024 left to head AI safety at the [US AI Safety Institute](/e/us-ai-safety-institute), which was renamed the Center for AI Standards and Innovation in 2025. His path, from EA-adjacent research to a lab to a lab's governance to government, maps the pipeline this story describes.
