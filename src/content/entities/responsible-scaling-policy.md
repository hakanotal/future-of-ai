[Anthropic](/e/anthropic)'s September 2023 framework, which sets "AI Safety Levels" modeled on biosafety labs and commits the company to stronger safeguards, or a pause, as its models cross defined danger thresholds. Rival labs published similar "frontier safety frameworks", and California's [SB 53](/e/sb-53) (2025) now requires large developers to publish theirs. It is the most developed form of [industry self-governance](/e/industry-self-governance), and it has the same weakness as all of them: the company writes, interprets and can revise its own rules.
