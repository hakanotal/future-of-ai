Existential-risk nonprofit co-founded in 2014 by [Jaan Tallinn](/e/jaan-tallinn), [Max Tegmark](/e/max-tegmark) and others. In 2015 [Elon Musk](/e/elon-musk) gave it $10 million for AI-safety research. In March 2023 it published the open letter [Pause Giant AI Experiments](/e/fli-pause-letter), calling for a six-month halt on training systems more powerful than GPT-4; thousands signed, no lab paused, and Yudkowsky replied that a pause was not enough. [Émile P. Torres](/e/emile-torres) once wrote for it, before turning critic.
