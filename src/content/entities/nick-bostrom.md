Swedish philosopher who founded the [Future of Humanity Institute](/e/future-of-humanity-institute) in 2005. His [Superintelligence](/e/superintelligence) (2014) gave academic shape to AI existential-risk concerns and hit Silicon Valley like an earthquake, [Musk](/e/elon-musk) began calling AI an existential threat after reading it.

Bostrom sits at the junction of [transhumanism](/e/transhumanism) and [longtermism](/e/longtermism): the framework imagining radically enhanced future minds also motivates preventing extinction, priced via [astronomical waste](/e/astronomical-waste) at up to 10⁵⁸ lives. In January 2023 he apologized for a 1996 message ranking Black people as less intelligent, an apology critics read as confirmation of the genealogical concern. FHI closed in 2024 after nineteen years.
