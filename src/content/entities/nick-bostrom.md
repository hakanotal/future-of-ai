Swedish philosopher (born 1973) whose ideas run through more TESCREAL letters than anyone's. He posted to the Extropians mailing list in the 1990s ([extropianism](/e/extropianism)), co-founded the World Transhumanist Association ([transhumanism](/e/transhumanism)), founded Oxford's [Future of Humanity Institute](/e/future-of-humanity-institute) in 2005, named [existential risk](/e/existential-risk) (2002), and wrote [Astronomical Waste](/e/astronomical-waste-2003) (2003) and [Superintelligence](/e/superintelligence) (2014), the book that made Musk and the founders of the labs fear AI.

His later work turns to what comes after: [Why I Want to Be a Posthuman When I Grow Up](/e/posthuman-essay-2008) and [Deep Utopia](/e/deep-utopia) (2024), which asks where humans would find meaning in a "solved world". In January 2023 he [apologized](/e/bostrom-email-apology) for a 1996 email containing a racist claim and a slur. In April 2024 Oxford [closed FHI](/e/fhi-closure). To admirers he is the philosopher who saw the biggest questions first; to critics he is the clearest link between the bundle's genealogy and its present.
