The February 2025 summit in Paris, co-chaired by France and India. Sixty signatories, including China, India, Japan and Canada, backed a declaration on "inclusive and sustainable" AI; the US and UK did not. US Vice President JD Vance told delegates that excessive regulation could kill a transformative industry. Three days later the UK renamed its AI Safety Institute the [AI Security Institute](/e/uk-ai-security-institute). If Bletchley was the high point of [AI safety](/e/ai-safety) as an international project, Paris marked the turn to the [race](/e/ai-race) and to [security](/e/ai-security).
