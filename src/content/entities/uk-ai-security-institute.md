The UK government body launched as the AI Safety Institute at the Bletchley Park summit in November 2023, the first state institute dedicated to testing frontier models. On 14 February 2025, three days after the Paris summit, the Technology Secretary announced at the Munich Security Conference that it would become the AI Security Institute, focused on serious risks with security implications such as cyberattacks and fraud. The one-word change is this story's turning point in miniature: from [safety](/e/ai-safety) to [security](/e/ai-security), from the lab's vocabulary to the state's.
