Netscape co-founder and co-founder of the venture firm [Andreessen Horowitz](/e/andreessen-horowitz) (a16z). His October 2023 [Techno-Optimist Manifesto](/e/techno-optimist-manifesto) argues that technology solves material problems, that energy use should rise a thousandfold, and that "existential risk", "sustainability" and "tech ethics" are among its enemies. It names "Beff Jezos", founder of [e/acc](/e/effective-accelerationism), among its patron saints.

For several weeks in 2023 his X bio read "cyberpunk activist; embracer of variance; TESCREAList": he adopted the critics' label as a badge. He is the accelerationist pole of the bundle's schism, at war with the [doomers](/e/doomerism) over the same premise: AGI is the most important thing happening. By 2025 his view that regulation costs lives was close to US federal policy ([AI Action Plan](/e/ai-action-plan-2025)).
