Not the rationalism of Descartes: the community built by [Eliezer Yudkowsky](/e/eliezer-yudkowsky) around [Overcoming Bias](/e/overcoming-bias) (2006), [LessWrong](/e/lesswrong) (2009) and later [Slate Star Codex](/e/slate-star-codex) (2013). The stated aim is identifying biases and deciding better; the practical focus is overwhelmingly AI danger.

The on-ramps were Yudkowsky's [Sequences](/e/sequences) and the fanfiction [HPMOR](/e/hpmor). The movement's own claim, unusual skill at reasoning, is what makes its epistemic failures fair game: the safe harbour for scientific racism and neoreaction ([Lewis-Kraus](/e/gideon-lewis-kraus)), the weaponization of logic against boundaries ([Joseph](/e/sonia-joseph); [Shen](/e/rochelle-shen) on discouraging third-party justice), and eventually the [Zizians](/e/zizians).

The steel-man stands: many rationalists reject Yudkowsky's doom, the community is genuinely divided, and members' crimes don't refute ideas by themselves.
