The US AI Safety Institute, created inside NIST in 2023, where [Paul Christiano](/e/paul-christiano) became head of AI safety in April 2024. In June 2025 Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick rebuilt it as the Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI), to focus on "demonstrable risks, such as cybersecurity, biosecurity, and chemical weapons", saying that "censorship and regulations have been used under the guise of national security" and that "innovators will no longer be limited by these standards". The rename captures a shift in [who decides](/e/ai-governance): from a safety mission shaped by researchers to a security-and-competitiveness mission shaped by the state's priorities in the [race](/e/ai-race).
