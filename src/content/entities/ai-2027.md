A 2025 scenario by [Daniel Kokotajlo](/e/daniel-kokotajlo), who left OpenAI over safety concerns, with [Scott Alexander](/e/scott-alexander) and colleagues. It narrates month by month how AI research automates itself: hundreds of thousands of superhuman AI researchers by 2027, then superintelligence. It offers two endings, a race to catastrophe or a coordinated slowdown. It went viral through the *New York Times*, the BBC and YouTube. Critics attacked its timelines; its authors say it is a scenario, not a prediction. Its real effect was to make the [intelligence explosion](/e/intelligence-explosion) feel like a policy question with a date on it, and so to strengthen the case for treating AI as a matter of [security](/e/ai-security).
