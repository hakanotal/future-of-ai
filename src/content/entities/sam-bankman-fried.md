MIT physics undergraduate turned [Jane Street](/e/jane-street) trader turned founder of [Alameda Research](/e/alameda-research) (2017) and [FTX](/e/ftx) (2019), the movement's model [earning-to-give](/e/earning-to-give) citizen until roughly $8 billion in customer deposits evaporated in the [November 2022 collapse](/e/ftx-collapse). Convicted on all counts in November 2023, sentenced to 25 years in March 2024.

Days after the fall he told [Vox](/e/kelsey-piper) the ethics had been mostly a front, right shibboleths for social acceptance. [Torres](/e/emile-torres) offers the darker reading: on strict utilitarian longtermism, a little fraud funding vast future good is not merely permitted but obligatory. [MacAskill](/e/william-macaskill) answered hubris; the open question is whether the hubris is incidental to the framework or generated by it.
