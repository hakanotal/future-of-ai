The Distributed AI Research Institute, founded by [Timnit Gebru](/e/timnit-gebru) in December 2021, a year after Google forced her out. It is deliberately distributed and community-rooted, studying AI's present-day harms ([AI ethics](/e/ai-ethics)) and imagining alternatives. Its *Possible Futures* series, "an imaginative space where near-future speculative storytelling meets grounded social critique", includes [Nyalleng Moorosi](/e/nyalleng-moorosi)'s vision of technology grown from community needs, such as tools that strengthen existing informal food-trading networks. It runs on a small fraction of the budgets available to [AI safety](/e/ai-safety) organizations, which is part of the point its founder makes about who gets to decide what AI is for.
