AI researcher who began as a teenage extropian expecting benevolent superintelligence by 2020, then reversed within months of starting the [Singularity Institute](/e/singularity-institute): smart does not mean good, catastrophe is the default, no fallback exists. That reversal became [AI alignment](/e/ai-alignment), pursued through [MIRI](/e/machine-intelligence-research-institute), [LessWrong](/e/lesswrong), the [Sequences](/e/sequences) and [HPMOR](/e/hpmor).

His network effects are enormous: [Thiel's](/e/peter-thiel) funding, the [Singularity Summit](/e/singularity-summit), [DeepMind's](/e/deepmind) seed, [Altman's](/e/sam-altman) account that Yudkowsky got many of them into AGI. His 2023 *TIME* op-ed put the default outcome bluntly, literally everyone on Earth dies, with airstrikes on rogue datacentres as enforcement; [If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies](/e/if-anyone-builds-it-everyone-dies) (2025, with [Soares](/e/nate-soares)) extends the case. Critics read the [dust-specks](/e/torture-vs-dust-specks) calculus as the whole method in miniature.
