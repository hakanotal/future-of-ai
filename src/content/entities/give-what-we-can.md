Giving society founded at Oxford in 2009 by [Toby Ord](/e/toby-ord), [William MacAskill](/e/william-macaskill) and [Bernadette Young](/e/bernadette-young). It began with 23 members, each pledging at least 10% of their income to the most effective charities they could find; it now counts many thousands of members. It is EA as a *practice*, distributed and grassroots, in contrast to EA as an *institution*, where a few funders set the agenda. Both are real, and much of the argument about effective altruism is about which one it is.
