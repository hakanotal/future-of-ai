Russian librarian and philosopher (1829–1903), the founder of Russian Cosmism. Fedorov taught that humanity's "common task" was to defeat death, physically resurrect every ancestor who had ever lived, and settle the cosmos to make room for them all. He influenced Tsiolkovsky, the father of Soviet rocketry. [Cosmism](/e/cosmism), the "C" in TESCREAL, is his idea with the theology removed, or, critics say, with the theology hidden ([techno-millenarianism](/e/techno-millenarianism)).
