A being whose basic capacities (healthspan, intelligence, emotional range) so exceed ours that it is no longer human in any ordinary sense. It is the destination of [transhumanism](/e/transhumanism). Bostrom's [essay](/e/posthuman-essay-2008) asks you to imagine it from the inside, the way a child might try to imagine being an adult, and argues that it could be very good. **A question to take away:** if your descendants became posthuman, would humanity have been saved or ended?
