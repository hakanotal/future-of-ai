Berkeley nonprofit founded by [Eliezer Yudkowsky](/e/eliezer-yudkowsky) in 2000 as the [Singularity Institute for Artificial Intelligence](/e/singularity-institute), funded by extropian entrepreneurs and later [Peter Thiel](/e/peter-thiel), and renamed MIRI in 2013. Its original goal was to *build* a friendly superintelligence; after Yudkowsky's reversal it became the home of [AI alignment](/e/ai-alignment) theory. By the 2020s, led by [Nate Soares](/e/nate-soares), it had concluded that alignment would not be solved in time and began arguing for an international halt to frontier development ([doomerism](/e/doomerism)). Few institutions have changed their minds about their own mission so completely, twice.
