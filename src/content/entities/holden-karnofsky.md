Co-founder of [GiveWell](/e/givewell) (2007, with [Elie Hassenfeld](/e/elie-hassenfeld), after both left the hedge fund Bridgewater) and of [Open Philanthropy](/e/open-philanthropy) (2014), which he led for most of a decade. He is one of the people most responsible for turning EA's money toward AI.

Under his leadership Open Philanthropy gave [OpenAI](/e/openai) $30 million in 2017, a grant that came with a seat on OpenAI's board, which he held until 2021. It also funded [CSET](/e/cset) ($55 million, 2019) and much of the AI-safety field. He was among the EA leaders warned about Bankman-Fried in 2018 ([Alameda split](/e/alameda-2018-split)). He left Open Philanthropy, spent 2024 at the Carnegie Endowment, and joined [Anthropic](/e/anthropic) in January 2025, where his wife, [Daniela Amodei](/e/daniela-amodei), is president. Nothing improper follows from any of this. But it is a vivid picture of how small and dense the network of people deciding AI's future is.
