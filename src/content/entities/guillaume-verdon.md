Quantum-computing physicist who, under the pseudonym "Beff Jezos", formulated [effective accelerationism](/e/effective-accelerationism) (e/acc) in 2022 as a rival to [effective altruism](/e/effective-altruism). He later founded the hardware startup Extropic, a name that nods to [extropianism](/e/extropianism).

On the Lex Fridman podcast he argued that EA optimizes a hedonic loss function prone to "spurious minima", and that its institutions became "the perfect cover to acquire tons of power and capital". He proposes a thermodynamic measure of progress instead: energy captured, complexity grown. He concedes the same critique of power could apply to his own movement. Critics see in his framing the move they find everywhere in the bundle: a contested value choice presented as a law of physics.
