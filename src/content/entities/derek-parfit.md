Oxford philosopher (1942–2017) whose [Reasons and Persons](/e/reasons-and-persons) (1984) laid the foundations of [longtermist](/e/longtermism) population ethics. He argued that we can wrong future people even though our choices determine who they will be (the [non-identity problem](/e/non-identity-problem)); his example was nuclear waste left to leak on people centuries from now. He also found the [repugnant conclusion](/e/repugnant-conclusion), a puzzle about "more lives is better" that he never fully solved. A mentor figure to [Toby Ord](/e/toby-ord) and the Oxford founders of EA, he is the bridge between a seminar-room puzzle and a funding strategy.
