The FTX Foundation's grant vehicle, established early 2022 (public launch late February) under CEO [Beckstead](/e/nick-beckstead) with [Aschenbrenner](/e/leopold-aschenbrenner), [Balwit](/e/avital-balwit) and [Ramakrishnan](/e/ketan-ramakrishnan), advised by [MacAskill](/e/william-macaskill). By September 2022 it had committed roughly $160M to 100+ organizations: ~$36.5M to EA orgs, ~$32M to AI safety, $14M to the [CEA](/e/centre-for-effective-altruism), $11.2M to biosecurity (HelixNano, SecureBio), $1.25M to [ARC](/e/alignment-research-center).
