The FTX Foundation's grant vehicle, announced on 28 February 2022 to fund "ambitious projects to improve humanity's long-term prospects". It was run by [Nick Beckstead](/e/nick-beckstead) with [Leopold Aschenbrenner](/e/leopold-aschenbrenner), [Avital Balwit](/e/avital-balwit) and [Ketan Ramakrishnan](/e/ketan-ramakrishnan), and [William MacAskill](/e/william-macaskill) advised. It committed about $160 million within months, almost all of it to [longtermist](/e/longtermism) causes: AI safety, biosecurity, EA infrastructure. TIME reported grants of $13.9 million to the [Centre for Effective Altruism](/e/centre-for-effective-altruism) and $17.9 million to [Longview Philanthropy](/e/longview-philanthropy).

On 10 November 2022 the whole team resigned, saying there were "many committed grants that the Future Fund will be unable to honor". Grantees later faced clawback demands from the bankruptcy estate. For eight months, a crypto fortune was one of the largest funders of the future of AI, and the man who had pitched earning to give to its founder sat on its advisory team.
