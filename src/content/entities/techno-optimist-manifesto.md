[Marc Andreessen](/e/marc-andreessen)'s essay of 16 October 2023, published by [a16z](/e/andreessen-horowitz). It argues that there is no material problem technology cannot solve, that energy use should rise a thousandfold, and that slowing AI costs lives. It names as its enemies "existential risk", "sustainability", "tech ethics" and "trust and safety", and lists "Beff Jezos" among its patron saints ([e/acc](/e/effective-accelerationism)). It is the accelerationist pole of the bundle's schism, and a clear answer to [who decides](/e/ai-governance): builders and markets, with regulators kept out of the way.
