Co-founder and president of [Anthropic](/e/anthropic) (2021), previously at [OpenAI](/e/openai) and Stripe; sister of [Dario Amodei](/e/dario-amodei). She is married to [Holden Karnofsky](/e/holden-karnofsky), co-founder of GiveWell and Open Philanthropy, who joined Anthropic in 2025. The tie is recorded here, neutrally, because it links two of the institutions that did most to shape AI safety: its largest philanthropic funder and one of its leading labs.
