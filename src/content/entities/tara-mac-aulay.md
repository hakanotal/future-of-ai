Co-founder of [Alameda Research](/e/alameda-research) and former senior executive at the [Centre for Effective Altruism](/e/centre-for-effective-altruism). Mac Aulay left Alameda in the [2018 split](/e/alameda-2018-split) and, TIME reported in March 2023, warned EA leaders including [William MacAskill](/e/william-macaskill) that Bankman-Fried was dishonest and negligent. Four years before the [collapse](/e/ftx-collapse), the movement had a well-placed insider reporting what they had seen.
