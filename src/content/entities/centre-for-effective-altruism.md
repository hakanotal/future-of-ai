Oxford's umbrella body (2012) coordinating the movement's projects. Its CEO [Robinson](/e/zach-robinson) wrote in March 2025 that EA had spent two years in a defensive crouch, momentum stopped, survival without collapse. It took $14M from the [Future Fund](/e/ftx-future-fund) and later faced an external investigation over what leadership knew about [Alameda](/e/alameda-research) and when.

The CEA is where the movement's two hardest questions meet: who knew what before [November 2022](/e/ftx-collapse), and whether principles-first reform after FTX is real or reputational.
