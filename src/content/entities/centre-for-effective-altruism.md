The umbrella body of the [effective altruism](/e/effective-altruism) movement, incorporated at Oxford in 2012 by [William MacAskill](/e/william-macaskill) and others, now part of [Effective Ventures](/e/effective-ventures). It runs EA conferences, groups and the EA Forum. [Sam Bankman-Fried](/e/sam-bankman-fried) worked there briefly in 2017, and in 2022 it received $13.9 million from the [FTX Future Fund](/e/ftx-future-fund).

Its CEO, [Zach Robinson](/e/zach-robinson), wrote in March 2025 that EA "has been in a defensive crouch for much of the past two-plus years" and that, although it had "defied predictions that the collapse of FTX would cause the collapse of EA", its momentum had stalled. By early 2026 CEA reported 20–25% growth in engagement. The movement survived; what it will choose to be is still open.
