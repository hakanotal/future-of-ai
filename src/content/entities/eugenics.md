The twentieth-century program of improving populations through coercion, usually racist, sexist or ableist: Nazi sterilization and murder, California's 20,000 forced sterilizations between 1909 and 1979. Negative [eugenics](/e/eugenics) prevented the "unfit" from reproducing; positive [eugenics](/e/eugenics) encouraged the "desirable" to reproduce more.

The underlying assumption is that evolution means *improvement* rather than adaptation, and the unavoidable question is who decides what counts as improved. [Huxley](/e/julian-huxley) wanted deliberate, artificial selection to outrun natural selection; [transhumanism](/e/transhumanism) inherits the frame while disavowing the coercion.

Genealogy is not refutation. But the pattern of inherited reasoning, some people's intelligence counting for more, is what the TESCREAL critique asks readers to watch for.
