Philosopher and historian (they/them) who spent roughly a decade inside the world they now critique: raised in an evangelical household fascinated by the end times, later a transhumanist, [longtermist](/e/longtermism) and effective altruist who wrote for the [Future of Life Institute](/e/future-of-life-institute). By their own account, they turned against it around the late 2010s and were pushed out.

With [Timnit Gebru](/e/timnit-gebru) they coined [TESCREAL](/e/tescreal) and wrote [The TESCREAL Bundle](/e/tescreal-bundle-paper) (2024). Their book [Human Extinction](/e/human-extinction-torres) (2023) traces how Western thinkers have imagined, and sometimes welcomed, the end of humanity. Torres's formulations are deliberately sharp: transhumanism as ["eugenics on steroids"](/e/transhumanism); the [FTX collapse](/e/ftx-collapse) as longtermist logic followed to its end ([ends justify means](/e/ends-justify-means)); AI discourse trapped in a [utopia/apocalypse binary](/e/utopia-apocalypse-binary) that erases the present. Their insider history is what gives the critique its detail, and what critics say gives it its heat.
