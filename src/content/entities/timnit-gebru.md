Computer scientist, born in Addis Ababa to Eritrean parents, who fled war as a teenager, earned a Stanford PhD, co-founded Black in AI, and co-led Google's Ethical AI team until December 2020, when Google forced her out in the [dispute](/e/gebru-google-exit) over [On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots](/e/stochastic-parrots). A year later she founded the [Distributed AI Research Institute](/e/dair).

With [Émile P. Torres](/e/emile-torres) she coined [TESCREAL](/e/tescreal) to explain what she saw from inside the industry: vast sums for speculative catastrophe ([AI safety](/e/ai-safety)) and little for the harms already happening ([AI ethics](/e/ai-ethics)). Her sharpest argument is a paradox: the language of existential threat legitimizes building dangerous, unscoped systems, because *if we don't, someone else will* ([AI race](/e/ai-race)). Her own answer to [who decides](/e/ai-governance) starts with the people currently harmed and absent from the room.
