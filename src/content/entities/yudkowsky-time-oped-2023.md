[Eliezer Yudkowsky](/e/eliezer-yudkowsky)'s TIME essay of 29 March 2023, "Pausing AI Developments Isn't Enough. We Need to Shut it All Down", written in response to the [Future of Life Institute's pause letter](/e/fli-pause-letter), which he thought asked for far too little.

Its central sentence is usually quoted without its condition. In full: "Many researchers steeped in these issues, including myself, expect that the most likely result of building a superhumanly smart AI, under anything remotely like the current circumstances, is that literally everyone on Earth will die." He called for an indefinite worldwide moratorium on large training runs, a cap on computing power, and a willingness to enforce it, including by destroying a rogue data centre by airstrike.

It is the canonical statement of [doomerism](/e/doomerism), and it moved the most extreme position in the field into a mainstream magazine.

**A question to take away:** the op-ed asks governments to be ready to use force to stop a technology. Whether you find that prudent or alarming, it puts the question of [who decides](/e/ai-governance) in its starkest form.
