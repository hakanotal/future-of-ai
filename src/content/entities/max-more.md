British-born philosopher (born 1964) who coined [extropianism](/e/extropianism) in 1988, co-founded the [Extropy Institute](/e/extropy-institute) in 1992 with Tom Morrow, and later served as CEO of the cryonics organization [Alcor](/e/alcor) (2011–2020). His "Principles of Extropy" framed death as an enemy and continuous self-transformation as a duty. The Extropians mailing list he helped build was an early meeting place for many of the people who would shape the AI debate, from Bostrom to a teenage Yudkowsky.
