Investor (PayPal, Palantir, Founders Fund, early Facebook) who funded the infrastructure of the AI-risk milieu before it had a name. He backed Yudkowsky's [Singularity Institute](/e/singularity-institute) and the [Singularity Summit](/e/singularity-summit). After the 2010 summit, [Demis Hassabis](/e/demis-hassabis) got a minute with him, opened with chess, and won a meeting that made Thiel [DeepMind](/e/deepmind)'s first major investor (reported at about $2.25 million).

Gebru and Torres name him among the figures linked to more than one TESCREAL ideology. He also funds national-conservative politics and has been close to the neoreactionary writer [Curtis Yarvin](/e/curtis-yarvin). In a 2025 New York Times interview, asked whether he would prefer the human race to endure, he hesitated at length before answering; the moment circulated widely. Whatever he meant by it, the hesitation is the question this whole story asks.
