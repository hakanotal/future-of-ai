Oxford philosopher and founding director of the [Global Priorities Institute](/e/global-priorities-institute), where academic longtermism took shape. With [William MacAskill](/e/william-macaskill) she wrote [The Case for Strong Longtermism](/e/strong-longtermism-2021) (2021), which estimates at least 10^24 expected future beings and concludes that a tiny cut in extinction risk is worth ten quadrillion lives. The paper is also candid about its weak point: the worry that it is driven by tiny probabilities ([fanaticism](/e/fanaticism)).
