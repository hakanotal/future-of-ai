Co-founder of Ethereum (born 1994) and a major donor to longtermist and AI-safety causes. Gebru and Torres name him among the figures linked to more than one TESCREAL ideology. In November 2023 he proposed [d/acc](/e/d-acc), "defensive accelerationism", as a middle path between [e/acc](/e/effective-accelerationism) and [doomerism](/e/doomerism): keep building, but favor technologies that protect and decentralize over those that concentrate power. It is one of the few proposals from inside the bundle that treats [who decides](/e/ai-governance) as part of the technical question.
