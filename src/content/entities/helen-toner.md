Australian AI-policy researcher who worked at [Open Philanthropy](/e/open-philanthropy), then helped launch the Georgetown think tank [CSET](/e/cset) in 2019 as its director of strategy (she became its interim executive director in 2025). As a member of OpenAI's nonprofit board, she voted to remove Sam Altman in November 2023 and left the board when he returned ([board crisis](/e/openai-board-crisis)). She is often described as close to EA and AI-safety circles, and her career connects the funders, the security think tanks and lab governance: three of the places where decisions about AI's future actually get made.
