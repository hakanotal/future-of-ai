Estonian programmer (born 1972), co-founder of Skype and Kazaa, and one of the largest private funders of existential-risk work. He co-founded Cambridge's [CSER](/e/cser) (2012) and the [Future of Life Institute](/e/future-of-life-institute) (2014), was an early investor in [DeepMind](/e/deepmind), and funds the [Survival and Flourishing Fund](/e/survival-and-flourishing-fund). Gebru and Torres name him among the figures linked to more than one TESCREAL ideology. Like several people here, he invested in AI and in warnings about it at the same time.
