"On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots: Can Language Models Be Too Big?" by [Emily Bender](/e/emily-bender), [Timnit Gebru](/e/timnit-gebru), Angelina McMillan-Major and [Margaret Mitchell](/e/margaret-mitchell) (FAccT, March 2021). It warned that ever-larger language models carry environmental and financial costs, encode the biases of their training data, and produce fluent text without understanding, which invites people to trust them. Google's demand that its employee authors withdraw it led to [Gebru's exit](/e/gebru-google-exit) in December 2020 and Mitchell's firing in February 2021. Many of its warnings read as descriptions of the chatbot era that followed. It remains the founding text of [AI ethics](/e/ai-ethics) as a counterweight to [AI safety](/e/ai-safety).
