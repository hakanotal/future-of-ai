Deep-learning pioneer, OpenAI co-founder and its chief scientist, whose views on AI risk made him the board's safety voice. In November 2023 he voted to remove Sam Altman, then days later signed the employee letter demanding the board resign and publicly apologized ([board crisis](/e/openai-board-crisis)). In 2024 he left and founded [Safe Superintelligence Inc.](/e/safe-superintelligence), with the single stated goal of building safe superintelligence. His reversal in five days is the most human moment in the governance story: even the person with the vote could not hold the line.
