The backbone of the bundle: humanity should transcend its biological limits through technology, longer life, the end of disease, cognition-expanding interfaces, ultimately immortality and the posthuman.

The word was popularized in 1957 by [Julian Huxley](/e/julian-huxley), who also presided over the British [Eugenics](/e/eugenics) Society. Modern transhumanists reject the coercive program and speak of liberal [eugenics](/e/eugenics) or germinal choice, individual reproductive freedom. Whether that distinction survives radically unequal access to enhancement is the substance of the dispute: if [Neuralink](/e/neuralink) someday radically enhances cognition, who can afford the implant?

On [Torres](/e/emile-torres)'s reading, transhumanism goes further than [eugenics](/e/eugenics): not merely improving the race but transcending it, eugenics on steroids.
