Events that could permanently destroy humanity's long-term potential: unaligned AI, engineered pandemics, nuclear war. [Ord](/e/toby-ord)'s [Precipice](/e/the-precipice) puts existential catastrophe this century at roughly one in six, Russian-roulette odds, with our own creations leading the table.

The category does real work and real mischief. It focuses minds on genuine civilization-scale dangers; it also converts every budget question into a hostage negotiation with infinity, since any expenditure on the present must justify itself against trillions of hypothetical future lives.
